Quantum Computing · PyPI + Cross-Platform App

novum-qvm + QuantumLab

Pure-Python quantum circuit simulator implementing the four-layer Perlin-Fourier Quantum Virtual Simulation (PFQVS) framework, plus a Flutter-based playground deployed across web, Android, and iOS. Visual circuit builder, 3D Bloch sphere, 15 guided lessons covering OpenQASM. pip install novum-qvm

Arabic Linguistics · Bilingual Site · Live

انهف — Arabic Neopronoun System

Bilingual (Arabic / English) reference site for انهف, a new gender-neutral Arabic pronoun for speakers who don't identify with existing feminine, masculine, or plural categories. Includes full conjugation rules across attached object markers, prepositions, possessives, and verb prefixes.
